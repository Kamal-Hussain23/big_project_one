# Mission

## Why this project exists

People struggle to find and book hotel rooms easily, especially when comparing
availability, prices, and dates across different options. **RoomReady** exists
to make booking a hotel room simple and clear.

## Who it is for

Travelers — tourists, business people, and families — who want to search
available rooms, see prices, and make a reservation without the hassle of phone
calls or confusing websites.

## What success looks like

A user can open the app, see which rooms are available for the dates they want,
pick a room at a fair price, and book it in a few simple steps. The booking is
stored so both the user and the hotel know the room is taken.

## Non-negotiables

- The app is simple to use and simple to read — no clever tricks.
- Plain, vanilla tools only: HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and basic Python (Flask).
- The project stays small and focused on booking rooms — no extra features that
  bloat the app.
- We use a simple database (SQLite) to store rooms and bookings.

## Explicitly out of scope

- Real payments or credit-card handling.
- Building a full hotel-management system for staff.
- Complicated search filters, reviews, loyalty programs, or accounts with
  passwords.
